---
title: Morning in Bogota
date: 2026-02-02
type: poems
excerpt: A small poem about the hour before the city fully wakes up.
published: true
---

Before the buses harden the avenue,
the city speaks in softer machinery.
Coffee.
A gate sliding open.
Someone rinsing yesterday from the sidewalk.

The mountains keep their distance.
Clouds move low enough
to feel borrowed.

For a minute,
everything unfinished
looks almost deliberate.
