---
title: A Note on Keeping Notes
date: 2026-03-30
type: notes
excerpt: Notes are where unfinished thoughts stay alive long enough to become useful.
published: true
---

Most ideas do not arrive ready for an essay.

They arrive half-formed, slightly embarrassed, and easy to lose. Notes give them a place to wait without demanding that they become something larger immediately.

That is reason enough to keep them.
